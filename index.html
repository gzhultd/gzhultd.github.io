Our
Products

Alongside our consultancy work, we build and operate our own products for New Zealand businesses.

Open source · MIT licensed

gate — PII redaction for AI agents

Gate intercepts database queries and AI tool calls, redacting names, emails, and AU/NZ identifiers like IRD, NHI, and TFN numbers before they reach the model. Installs as a Claude Code hook in minutes — no code changes or data migration required.

Jielong — batch commerce for NZ merchants

Jielong lets group-buy sellers open a campaign window, collect demand, and produce once. Reusable product catalogues, multi-location pickup slots, automatic production summaries, and bank-transfer payments via Akahu — shareable straight into WeChat.
